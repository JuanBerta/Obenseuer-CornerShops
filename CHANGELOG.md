# Changelog

## 0.2.0
- Shop stock, vendor cash and restock day are saved with your game (per character).
- Sort button on the shop screen (uses Sort By's order when installed).
- Special Orders 1.0.6 can order from the corner shops.

## 0.1.0
- First release: Arvid Vinter, Carl Hagen and Joran Kallio sell food, drinks, utility and paperwork items through the game's trade screen (Shift+E while looking at them). Daily restock; configurable vendors, stock, prices and cash.
