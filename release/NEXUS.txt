=== NAME ===
Corner Shops

=== SHORT DESCRIPTION (summary) ===
The hotel and motel keepers sell everyday supplies: food, drinks, can openers, matches, batteries, paper, envelopes, stamps and more. Shift+E while looking at them opens the game's own trade screen.

=== CATEGORY ===
Gameplay (or Miscellaneous)

=== FILE DESCRIPTION ===
Obenseuer-CornerShops-v0.2.0.rar : Corner Shops 0.2.0 for BepInEx 6. Extract into the Obenseuer folder.

=== CHANGELOG (Nexus "Changelogs" tab) ===
0.2.0
- The shops are saved: what's on the shelf (including what you sold them), their cash and the restock day are kept in your save. A new game or another character gets its own shops.
- Sort button on the shop screen. With Sort By installed it uses your chosen order (the button shows it).
- Special Orders support: with Special Orders 1.0.6 the vendors take orders for anything on their stock list.
0.1.0
- First release.

=== FULL DESCRIPTION (BBCode) ===
[size=5][b]Corner Shops[/b][/size]
[i]Running out of bread or matches shouldn't mean a trip across the city.[/i]

The people who run the hotels now keep a little shop behind the counter:
[list]
[*][b]Arvid Vinter[/b]
[*][b]Carl Hagen[/b] at Hotel Korso (Bazaar)
[*][b]Joran Kallio[/b] at The Pickaxe Motel (mines)
[/list]

[size=4][b]How to use[/b][/size]
Look at one of them: the prompt shows [b](Talk)   Shift+E: Shop[/b]. Press [b]Shift+E[/b] and the game's own trade screen opens with their stock. Plain [b]E[/b] still talks to them as before.

[size=4][b]What they sell[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Food:[/b] rye, white and multigrain bread, canned meat, tuna, sardines, pea and tomato soup, peas, peaches, Lenkki sausages, potato chips, chocolate and candy bars.
[*][b]Drinks:[/b] Moca-Cola, Splurt, Fail Soda, Ice Tea, milk.
[*][b]Utility:[/b] can opener, matchbox, lighter, candles, AA and D batteries, toilet paper, soap, bandages, painkillers, duct tape, glue.
[*][b]Paperwork:[/b] paper, notebook, envelopes, stamps, ink, fancy paper.
[/list]
The stock refills every in-game day and is kept in your save. They also buy things from you, with a daily cash amount, just like the game's traders. A [b]Sort[/b] button on the shop screen tidies their shelf.

[size=4][b]Built on the game's own trading[/b][/size]
Buying, selling, prices, the wealth line and the stolen-item rules are the game's own trader system, so everything behaves like any other trader. Items you buy are yours, not stolen.

[size=4][b]Configuration[/b][/size]
In [i]BepInEx/config/com.juanberta.obenseuer.cornershops.cfg[/i]:
[list]
[*][b]Vendors[/b]: which NPCs run a shop (add any NPC by name).
[*][b]ShopKey[/b]: Shift+E by default.
[*][b]Items[/b]: the stock list, as [i]Item name:amount[/i] separated by commas, with item names as the game shows them.
[*][b]BuyPriceFactor[/b] (1.25), [b]SellPriceFactor[/b] (0.6), [b]BuysFromPlayer[/b] (on), [b]VendorCash[/b] (2000 OC a day).
[/list]

[size=4][b]Notes[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Works with [b]Special Orders[/b]: order anything they stock in bulk, delivered by mail.
[*]Works with [b]Sort By[/b]: the shop's Sort button uses your chosen order.
[*]Works well with [b]Bank Inventory[/b] (its storage search works in the shop) and [b]Diegetic UI[/b] (paper look).
[*]Requires BepInEx 6 (be.697 or newer).
[/list]

[size=4][b]Installation[/b][/size]
Extract the archive into the Obenseuer game folder. The DLL ends up in [i]BepInEx/plugins/Obenseuer-CornerShops/[/i].
